## F-013: single-pin labels fixed at the root
- Root cause: the S-row wire holes (Q row) were duplicate pads 14–20 of the module footprint. The labels S2..S6 therefore had a single schematic pin each, and the project ignored that check globally.
- Fix: the Q row is now its own part, J3 (Conn_01x07, footprint FrutaMIDI:WireHoles_1x07_P2.54mm: 7 × Ø1.8 mm pads, 0.9 mm drill, silkscreen S1..S7, excluded from the BOM). Each label S1..S7 now joins A2 and J3 (S1 and S7 also join JP1).
- The module courtyard is back to a plain rectangle. The net names S1..S7 are unchanged, so the frozen interface is unaffected.
- In frutamidi.kicad_pro, `isolated_pin_label` changed from "ignore" to "warning" (the KiCad default). Since the build runs with --severity-all, warnings fail it.
- ERC: "ERC messages: 0  Errors 0  Warnings 0". The report's ignored-check list no longer contains "Label connected to only one pin". No ignore or exclusion was added.

## F-014: terminal drill and pot slot
- The terminal block drill is now 1.4 mm (was 1.3), with a 3.0 mm pad, so the ring is 0.80 mm. The fit gap to the 0.9 × 0.9 mm pin (diagonal 1.27 mm, Phoenix data EE-S12, search) is 0.13 mm. CALCULATION. pcb_rules_check: "min ring 0.80 mm (pad 1 rect 3x3 drill 1.4)".
- Pin sections:
  - Phoenix MKDS 1,5: 0.9 × 0.9 mm (search, EE-S12; the official PDF was not opened).
  - Alps RK09K: NOT sourced. The dimension drawing on the Alps page is an image. The terminal size stays ASSUMPTION (about 0.8 × 0.5 mm); the 1.0 mm holes are PARTIAL with a 0.06 mm fit gap.
- The pot mounting tabs ("MP" pads) are oval slots: 2.1 × 1.8 mm drill, 4 × 3 mm pad. Proposed instruction for the docs: "Drill the two potentiometer tab holes with a 1.8 mm bit, then widen each one along the long side of its pad (left-right, the 4 mm direction) to about 2.1 mm with a round needle file or by rocking the 1.8 mm bit sideways. Test-fit the pot before soldering; the tabs must drop in without force." The tab dimensions are not sourced: test-fit before widening.

## F-015: BOM fields (bom-kicad.csv)
- A1: Raspberry Pi SC0915 (= Pico, without headers). SOURCE EE-S21, distributor listings.
- A2: Adafruit 1982 (product number, EE-S04).
- J2: Phoenix Contact 1715721 (MKDS 1,5/ 2-5,08) ×7.
  - The review's 1729128 is, per distributor listings, the **MKDSN** 1,5/2-5,08 (13.5 A). That is a different series from the MKDS 1,5 model the footprint and its pin data refer to (EE-S22), so I used 1715721.
  - The quantity per board is 7 blocks. The BOM row shows Qty 1 (one symbol) with "x7" in the MPN.
- RV1: Alps Alpine, "RK09K series, 10k linear (B), vertical". The exact order code was not sourced: RK09K1130A5R is 10 kΩ (EE-S13), but its taper was not confirmed.
- SW1: Omron B3F-1000 (EE-S23, search). Omron shows a 6.5 × 4.5 mm PCB pattern with Ø1.0 holes, matching the footprint, but whether that drawing covers this variant is not confirmed.
- Female headers are BOM-only symbols (on_board = no, all pins no-connect, no footprint): J4 and J5 1×20, J6 1×13, J7 1×07, all "generic 2.54 mm single-row female header". No maker/MPN sourced.
- Not added, for the README: the SC0915 has no headers, so it needs 2 × 1×20 male 2.54 mm headers soldered to the Pico. The Adafruit 1982 is normally supplied with a male header strip (not verified in this task).
