## Open decisions for manager
1. **C2 was not added.** No GND copper exists right of x ≈ 45 mm above the SDA run (y 21 mm). The only single-layer route needs two things:
   - a 3V3 branch between J1 and the H2 fixing metal, within about 0.05 mm of the assumed washer;
   - a GND run of about 48 mm under the module, 1.1 mm from all 12 ELE pins.

   The cap-to-module loop would be about 100 mm long (about 100 nH, ASSUMPTION), so it would do little decoupling. Both module revisions already have their own 3.3 V LDO (EE-S02/S03), which JP1 feeds. Options: (a) accept without C2; (b) accept that route anyway; (c) put a decoupling cap at the Pico instead (also blocked: no GND crossing at the 3V3 x = 25 mm drop). Recommendation: (a).
2. **Systems update needed:** interface_check `pcb.A1.AGND_isolated` must allow C1 on AGND (and RV1 MP).
3. **MPN correction:** use C322C104K5R5TA, not C320C104K5R5TA, wherever the BOM or budgets mention C1.
4. **Budget:** sim/systems/budgets (REQ-032) should add C1, 0.20–0.90 €.
5. **metrics.json:** I did not update management/metrics.json (outside my paths). Numbers to record: JP1 gap 1.87 mm; J2 pad 3.0 × 4.5 mm; C1 τ worst 0.38 ms with a 10.4× margin.
