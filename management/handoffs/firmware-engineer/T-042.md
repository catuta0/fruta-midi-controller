## Assumptions (unchanged from T-041; will become SOURCE once the datasheet is read)
- SCTS is bit 7 of AUTOCONFIG1 (0x7C). AUTOCONFIG0 (0x7B) holds FFI[7:6], RETRY[5:4], BVA[3:2], ARE[1] and ACE[0].
- The MPR121 auto-increments the register address on a multi-byte read. The burst reads span 0x04–0x1B or 0x1D (filtered) and 0x1E–0x29 (baseline).
- The formula is USL = (VDD − 0.7)/VDD × 256, TL = 0.9 × USL, LSL = 0.65 × USL.
