**I2C margins for electronics' calc_i2c_series** (CALCULATION)

| Case | Level | Limit | Margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pico drives low, MPR121 receives | bus 0.742 V (0.5 V + 1.10 mA × 220 Ω), plus 0.05 V level-shifter drop (ASSUMPTION) | VIL 0.3 × 3.10 V = 0.930 V | **0.138 V** |
| MPR121 drives low (VOL 0.5 V, S-04), Pico receives | pad 0.565 V | VIL 0.8 V | **0.235 V** |
| High at the Pico | 3.3 V | VIH 2.0 V | 1.30 V |
| High at the MPR121 (module side pulled to its own VDD) | VDD | 0.7 × VDD | ≥ 0.93 V |

- Fault current: 3.4 V / 220 Ω = 15.5 mA per line, 30.9 mA for both. Within 50 mA.
- The 220 Ω × 10 pF pad capacitance (ASSUMPTION) gives 2.2 ns, negligible.
- The 0.138 V margin assumes VOL at its 0.5 V maximum. That maximum is specified at the drive-setting current (default 4 mA, ASSUMPTION), while the actual current is about 1.1 mA. So the real margin is likely larger, but that is not sourced.

**I2C rise time** (CALCULATION, RPU 80 kΩ = slowest edge)
- 156 ns at 60 pF; C_b limit 115.2 pF.
- Breadboard without the 4.7 kΩ pull-ups: 452 ns, failing above 39.8 pF.

**Burst plan** (CALCULATION, `fm_mpr121.h`)
- Worst case: 42 bus bytes in 8 transactions (mask 0x925). The old per-pad path took 45 B. All 12 pads: 24 B + 12 B.
- The script asserts the header's "at most 42 bytes" over all 4095 masks.

**Latency at 400 kHz** (CALCULATION, C ≤ 100 pF)
- 1 sensor: worst 10.59 ms, jitter 6.59 ms, worst loop 1.59 ms.
- 2 sensors: worst 12.07 ms, jitter 8.07 ms.
- Design limit C ≤ 210 pF.
