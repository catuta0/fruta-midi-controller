\paragraph{What did not change in v1.2.} Firmware, pins and build flags; the J2
terminal row and pinout; the I\textsuperscript{2}C, USB MIDI and terminal
interfaces apart from the R1--R12 part, C1, the AGND members and the JP1 and J2
footprints (ADR-021); the resistor value
(\qty{3.3}{\kilo\ohm} 1\,\%) and therefore the electrode sensitivity analysis; the
home-etch design rules (ADR-019 D-4, \ev{management/INTERFACES.json}).

a51dfff (PASS 17, PARTIAL 18, FAIL 3, NOT RUN 4). REQ-023 stays PARTIAL (no ESD-gun
test); REQ-006 stays PARTIAL (C1 settling by calculation only); REQ-032 stays FAIL with
a larger overrun (8.62\,EUR instead of 0.40\,EUR, \lbl{CALCULATION})
\ev{management/REQUIREMENTS.md}.

PASS 18, PARTIAL 18, FAIL 2, NOT RUN 4. REQ-032 moved from FAIL to PASS on the
ADR-027 D-1 basis; REQ-036 moved from PASS to FAIL while the documents followed the
board (draft-10) and back to PASS in draft-14 after T-105 (document consistency
only); REQ-023 stays PARTIAL \ev{management/REQUIREMENTS.md}.
