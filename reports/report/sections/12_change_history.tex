\paragraph{What did not change in v1.2.} Firmware, pins and build flags; the J2
terminal row and pinout; the I\textsuperscript{2}C, USB MIDI and terminal
interfaces apart from the R1--R12 part; the resistor value
(\qty{3.3}{\kilo\ohm} 1\,\%) and therefore the electrode sensitivity analysis; the
home-etch design rules (ADR-019 D-4, \ev{management/INTERFACES.json}).

\paragraph{Requirement status after CR-002.} Unchanged counts in REQUIREMENTS draft-7 at
c901ad8 (PASS 17, PARTIAL 18, FAIL 3, NOT RUN 4); the systems update T-074 was in
progress when this report was built. REQ-023 stays PARTIAL (no ESD-gun test); REQ-032 stays FAIL with
a larger overrun (7.72\,EUR instead of 0.40\,EUR, \lbl{CALCULATION})
\ev{management/REQUIREMENTS.md}.
