\subsection{Electrode path and ESD (critical block)}
Model \nolinkurl{sim_electrode.py} (\lbl{SIMULATION}): constant-current charge into
a 17\,pF pin plus series $R$ plus external $C$, swept over $R$ = 0--100\,k$\Omega$
and $C$ = 10--2000\,pF; the discharge model is an \lbl{ASSUMPTION}
\ev{reports/electronics/analysis.md}.

\begin{longtable}{@{}lrrrl@{}}
\toprule
Variant & Peak 4\,kV & Peak 8\,kV & Sensitivity & Verdict \\
\midrule\endhead
a) none & 15.0\,A & 30.0\,A & 1.000 & FAIL (11$\times$ over) \\
\textbf{b) 3.3\,k$\Omega$ series (selected)} & 1.10\,A & 2.20\,A & 0.904 & Estimate at 4\,kV (review F-002), PARTIAL 8\,kV \\
b') 10\,k$\Omega$ series & 0.39\,A & 0.77\,A & 0.679 & FAIL (sensitivity) \\
c) 3.3\,k$\Omega$ + TPD4E05U06 & 1.10\,A & 2.20\,A & $\approx$0.90 & FAIL (SMD constraint) \\
d) 3.3\,k$\Omega$ + 2$\times$1N4148 & 1.10\,A & 2.20\,A & $\approx$0.90 & PARTIAL (12 jumpers) \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item ESD model: IEC 150\,pF/330\,$\Omega$; pin limit 1.33\,A inferred from HBM
        2\,kV (\lbl{ASSUMPTION}). Minimum $R$: 2.67\,k$\Omega$ at 4\,kV,
        5.67\,k$\Omega$ at 8\,kV. Design rule 2.7--3.3\,k$\Omega$; chosen
        \qty{3.3}{\kilo\ohm} (Yageo MFR-12FTF52-3K3).
  \item With 3.3\,k$\Omega$ the smallest detectable touch is 0.5\,pF on 10\,pF and
        19.9\,pF on 1000\,pF (19.8\,pF without $R$) (\lbl{SIMULATION}).
  \item Resistor pulse rating (T-053/T-054): the peak across $R$ is 3636\,V at
        4\,kV and 7273\,V at 8\,kV (\lbl{CALCULATION})
        \ev{sim/electronics/calc/calc_pulse.out}. The MFR-12 is rated 400\,V
        maximum overload with no microsecond pulse rating: FAIL against the
        datasheet. No candidate with a datasheet-confirmed $\ge$3.64\,kV rating
        was found (ADR-012, CR-001). REQ-023 stays PARTIAL.
  \item The breadboard build has no electrode protection beyond the MPR121's
        internal structures (HBM $\pm$2\,kV, \lbl{SOURCE} EE-S01), because the module
        wires ELE0--11 straight to its header (T-055).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{I\textsuperscript{2}C bus}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item At e4668d4 the rise time with only the module \qty{10}{\kilo\ohm}
        pull-ups was 322\,ns at 38\,pF against 300\,ns: FAIL (\lbl{CALCULATION},
        bus C partly \lbl{ASSUMPTION}). With R13/R14 \qty{4.7}{\kilo\ohm}: 103\,ns
        at 38\,pF (electronics) and 156\,ns at 60\,pF (systems, RPU 80\,k$\Omega$
        \lbl{SOURCE} S-17): PASS (\lbl{CALCULATION}) \ev{reports/systems/budgets.md}.
  \item Series R15/R16 \qty{220}{\ohm}: fault current \qty{15.5}{\milli\ampere}
        per line, \qty{30.9}{\milli\ampere} for both, against \qty{50}{\milli\ampere}
        (\lbl{CALCULATION}). Low-level margins (authoritative, RP2040 datasheet levels
        \lbl{SOURCE} S-17): 0.138\,V when the Pico drives low (VIL 0.93\,V at the
        MPR121) and 0.235\,V when the MPR121 drives low (VIL 0.8\,V at the Pico):
        PASS (\lbl{CALCULATION}) \ev{reports/systems/budgets.md}. The earlier
        electronics figures (0.218\,V and 0.286\,V, with RP2040 levels as
        \lbl{ASSUMPTION}, T-055) are superseded interim values.
  \item Breadboard without the \qty{4.7}{\kilo\ohm} pull-ups: 452\,ns at 60\,pF,
        FAIL above 39.8\,pF; hence ADR-009 D-11 (\lbl{CALCULATION}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Safety statement}
Each PCB electrode has a \qty{3.3}{\kilo\ohm} series resistor. Its protection
against IEC 61000-4-2 contact discharge at $\pm$4\,kV is an estimate resting on
\lbl{ASSUMPTION}s (the pin limit is inferred from HBM 2\,kV), not a pass; it compares
peak current only, and the resistor itself is not pulse-rated for the resulting
voltage (review finding F-002 \ev{reports/review/72f67f0/findings.md}). Only an
ESD-gun test, with a pulse-rated resistor (CR-001), can close it. The design is not
evaluated beyond that, and not for touching while connected to mains-powered
equipment (ADR-003, ADR-012).
