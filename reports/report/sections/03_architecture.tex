\section{Architecture and decisions}
\label{sec:architecture}

\subsection{System overview}
Touch path: electrode object $\rightarrow$ lead $\rightarrow$ screw terminal
$\rightarrow$ \qty{3.3}{\kilo\ohm} series resistor (PCB) $\rightarrow$ MPR121
electrode pin; the MPR121 reports touch bits and filtered/baseline data over
I\textsuperscript{2}C at \qty{400}{\kilo\hertz} to the Pico, which sends USB MIDI 1.0
messages to the host (browser sampler or DAW)
\ev{management/INTERFACES.json} \ev{reports/systems/architecture_options.md}.
