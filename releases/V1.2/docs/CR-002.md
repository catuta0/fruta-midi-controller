# CR-002: Electrode series resistor flashover and pulse rating (FrutaMIDI v1.2)

- Requester: user (chat, 2026-11-05: "Faço essa v1.2? sim"), following the manager's PCB design review (analisar-pcb, 2026-11-04).
- Date opened: 2026-11-07. Baseline: V1_BASELINE (tag v1.1.0-baseline, 048023c); work branch `change/CR-002` from b1e4bf5.
- Supersedes candidate CR-001 (pulse-rated resistor): both change the same parts and footprint, so they are handled together.

## Reason
R1–R12 (3k3, DIN0204, vertical, 2.54 mm pad pitch) limit the ESD current into the MPR121 electrode pins (REQ-023).
1. Flashover across the resistor itself. Pad 1.6 mm on 2.54 mm pitch leaves 0.94 mm copper-to-copper between the ELEx and Ex pads (CALCULATION from the footprint). The vertical lead also runs about 1 mm from the opposite end cap (ASSUMPTION, typical vertical 0204 bend). At IEC 4 kV contact, almost the whole voltage appears across the resistor: 4000 V × 3300/3630 = 3.64 kV (CALCULATION). Air breakdown at about 3 kV/mm (ASSUMPTION, the value already used in reports/electronics/analysis.md) gives about 2.8 kV for 0.94 mm, so the arc can bypass the resistor. The MPR121 pin would then see the IEC source limited only by 330 Ω (about 12 A at 4 kV, CALCULATION). analysis.md said "each path still has its series R"; that is wrong for the gap across the resistor.
2. Pulse rating: Yageo MFR-12 has no rating ≥ 3.64 kV (ADR-012, CR-001 candidate).
